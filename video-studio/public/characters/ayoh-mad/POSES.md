# AYOH MAD — 8 pose briefs (project: "Di Sebalik Perjalanan Seorang Usahawan")

How to use: open the image tool, ATTACH `00-master/master.png`, paste **BASE** + one **POSE** block. One image per request (no collage).
Recommended size: portrait 1080×1920 (9:16) or at least 1024×1536, character fully inside the frame with space above the head.

## BASE (paste first, every time)
> Use the attached image as the exact character reference. Same person, same face shape, same eyes, eyebrows, nose and lips, same thick black hair with a centre parting and curtain fringe, same thin moustache and thin chin beard, same young neat look and body build. Same outfit: royal-blue crew-neck T-shirt with "LUTFI" in white on the left chest and the white circular "YPF – YA PAKCIK FROZEN" snowflake logo on the right chest, small black clip-on microphone at the collar. Same semi-realistic cartoon illustration style: clean dark line art, soft cel shading, gentle gradients. Single character, single image, not a collage. NO signboards, NO logos or text on walls, NO extra brand names anywhere in the background. Vertical 9:16 composition.

## POSE 1 — Membuka gerai awal pagi (sunyi, tekad)
> Pose: he is alone, pulling up the shutter / opening his small frozen-food stall in an empty covered market at dawn. Calm, determined expression. Light: soft blue-orange dawn light, long shadows, quiet atmosphere, empty market.

## POSE 2 — Mengangkat kotak stok seorang diri (penat, gigih)
> Pose: he carries a stack of plain cardboard boxes of frozen stock by himself, arms straining, walking towards the stall. Effortful but determined expression, slight sweat. Light: bright morning. Boxes are plain brown with no printed brand.

## POSE 3 — Membungkus pesanan di kaunter (fokus)
> Pose: he is packing customer orders at a white tiled counter, putting frozen packs into clear plastic bags, a small order list beside him. Focused expression, eyes on his hands. Light: daylight inside the market.

## POSE 4 — Penat waktu malam, telefon penuh mesej (letih, jujur)
> Pose: late at night, he sits on a stool at the closed stall, shoulders dropped, looking at a glowing phone screen full of order messages (no readable text). Tired, honest expression. Light: dark night, single warm lamp, phone glow on his face.

## POSE 5 — Termenung (renung, tenang)
> Pose: he stands by the counter looking into the distance, one hand resting on the counter, thinking deeply. Serious but calm, reflective expression. Light: golden late-afternoon light.

## POSE 6 — Senyum yakin, tangan bersilang (harapan, yakin)
> Pose: he stands in front of his stall with arms crossed, smiling confidently at the viewer. Proud, warm expression. Light: bright clean daylight.

## POSE 7 — Menunjuk ke arah penonton (ajakan)
> Pose: he points straight at the viewer with his right hand, friendly inviting expression, slight smile, as if saying "awak". Light: bright daylight, simple soft background.

## POSE 8 — Menghulur tangan, mengalu-alukan (hangat)
> Pose: he extends his open right hand towards the viewer for a handshake, welcoming warm smile. Light: warm soft daylight, simple background with depth of field.
